FitFlow Privacy Policy

Effective: 1 October 2026. This is a student-project draft; have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer before a commercial launch, and replace the example contact details.

1. Introduction

FitFlow ("we") provides a fitness and nutrition mobile app. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, how we protect it, and your rights.

2. Information we collect

  • Account data: first name and preferences you enter.
  • Health and fitness data: goals, level, workout history, meal logs and nutrition summaries.
  • Camera and photos: meal photos you choose to take or select for food recognition.
  • User content: posts, reactions and circle membership.
  • Device and usage data (only if you enable analytics): app version, device type, crash logs.

3. How we use information

To run the app, personalise your Daily Flow, operate private social circles, keep the community safe, fix bugs and comply with the law.

4. AI processing

Personalisation and food recognition run on your device where possible. Recommendations and recognition results are automated estimates for general wellness, not medical advice. You can turn personalisation off in Settings; plans then use only the answers you gave.

5. Social features

Circles are private and joined by invite code. Health metrics are never shared unless you choose to post them. You can report or block users and delete your posts.

6. Legal bases (GDPR) and consent

We rely on contract (to provide the app), explicit consent (health data, camera, optional analytics, withdrawable at any time), legitimate interests (security and improvement) and legal obligation.

7. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share data only with service providers under contract, with people you choose in circles, with authorities when legally required, or in a business transfer. Anonymised, aggregated trends may be shared with partners only if you opt in.

8. Retention

Data is kept while your account is active and deleted or anonymised within 30 days of account deletion, except where the law requires otherwise.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest where stored on our servers; access is restricted; we will notify users and regulators of breaches as required by law.

10. Your rights

Access and export (Settings, Export my data), correction, deletion (Settings, Delete account and data), restriction and objection, withdrawal of consent, and complaint to your data protection authority. California residents have CCPA/CPRA rights to know, delete, correct and opt out of sale or sharing; we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context advertising.

11. HIPAA

FitFlow is a consumer wellness app, not a covered entity under HIPAA. We apply HIPAA-aligned safeguards to health information.

12. Children

FitFlow is not for children under 13 (or the higher minimum age in your country).

13. Changes

We will post updates here and notify you in the app about material changes.
